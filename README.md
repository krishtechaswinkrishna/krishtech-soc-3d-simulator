# 🛡️ KrishTech-SOC-3D_Simulator

> **An immersive 3D Security Operations Center (SOC) simulation where players investigate cyberattacks, respond to incidents, and uncover insider threats.**

## 📌 Overview

**KrishTech-SOC-3D_Simulator** is a browser-based cybersecurity simulation game that places players in the role of a SOC analyst at KrishTech. Instead of learning through quizzes or static tutorials, players experience realistic cyber incidents inside an interactive 3D office environment.

From phishing emails and compromised accounts to insider threats and ransomware attacks, every decision influences the investigation and the organization's security.

## 🎮 Gameplay

As a newly appointed SOC analyst, your responsibilities include:

* Monitoring security alerts
* Investigating suspicious activities
* Interviewing employees
* Collecting digital evidence
* Responding to cyber incidents
* Identifying insider threats
* Protecting the organization's infrastructure
* Preventing a large-scale ransomware attack

Every investigation requires observation, logical reasoning, and cybersecurity knowledge.

## 🧩 Mystery Cases

Beyond the main campaign, the simulator includes detective-style cyber mystery cases where players investigate unique incidents using clues, evidence, and logical deduction.

Each case offers a different challenge and encourages critical thinking rather than memorization.
